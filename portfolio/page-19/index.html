No. 001             Shower Curtain for Correctional Housing

Sponsored by Bob Barker Company, this project was a collaboration with NC State University, designing a shower curtain for correctional housing that is safe, durable, and easy to maintain. The design process included research into the needs of incarcerated individuals, correctional staff, and maintenance personnel, as well as testing of materials and prototypes in real-world conditions.

No. 002             Drone Motor Testing

This setup was developed to test the output efficiency of a heavy lift drone engine for potential competition analysis. The research was conducted to evaluate the performance of the engine under different load conditions, and to identify potential areas for improvement. The research included both computational fluid dynamics (CFD) simulations and physical testing of prototypes.
